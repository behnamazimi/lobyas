Lobyas helps the terms of a field, or the words of a language, stick. Read a few every day, test them lightly, and keep what matters.

READ FIRST
Open a term, take it in, move on. There's no pass or fail. Reading comes first and testing comes second, so it feels closer to reading the news than to studying.

REVIEW AND QUIZ
Recall a term in Review, or check yourself with a Quiz every few days. Lobyas works out what you know from how well you remember each term, so you never mark anything as known by hand.

NO DUE DATES
There's no schedule and no backlog. Whatever you're most likely to forget comes up first, so coming back after a break is easy.

STORIES AND LISTEN
Turn the terms you're about to read into a short story, in the style and level you pick. Hear terms and stories read aloud. Stories and AI quizzes are optional and use AI credits; the rest of the app works without them.

YOUR OWN COLLECTIONS
Build collections, edit every field, export them, and share one with friends through a link.

GOALS AND STREAKS
Set a daily pace and keep a streak going, with a shield for the days you miss.

Lobyas is invite-only for now so it can stay small and well looked after. Request access at lobyas.com.
